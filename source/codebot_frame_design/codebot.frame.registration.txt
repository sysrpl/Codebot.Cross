The Codebot.Frame.Registration unit adds a Convert form or frame to control item to the Tools menu of the Lazarus IDE and to the right click menu of the form designer. The items are only enabled when the active editor tab has a form or frame open in the designer.

When you choose it, you are asked for the details of your new control with the dialog in Codebot.Frame.ConvertDialog. Your form is then saved and converted with Codebot.Frame.Converter, the package, unit, and resource file are written, and anything that could not be converted is listed in the Messages window. Finally, the new unit is opened and the package is installed, if you chose to.
