# Codebot.Render.Controls.Gtk3

The Codebot.Render.Controls.Gtk3 unit is the GTK3 widgetset class of TGraphicsBox. It creates the handle of the control and provides two native windows: the window of the control, which the SDL window is placed inside, and the window of the form that holds the control. It needs an X11 window, so it does not work under Wayland.
