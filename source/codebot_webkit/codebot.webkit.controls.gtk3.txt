The Codebot.WebKit.Controls.Gtk3 unit defines the GTK3 widgetset classes of the browser and inspector controls. TWSWebBrowser creates the web view and carries out the requests of the control, and TWSWebInspector holds the inspector. The signals of the web view reach the controls through IWebBrowserEvents and IWebInspectorEvents. A control has no web view at design time, or when WebKitGTK is not installed.
