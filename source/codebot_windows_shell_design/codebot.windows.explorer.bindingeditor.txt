The Codebot.Windows.Explorer.BindingEditor unit defines the form used to edit a TExplorerBinding at design time. The form lists the TExplorerView, TExplorerTree, TExplorerEdit, and TExplorerPreview controls of the binding's owner, and moves them between the available and bound lists. TExplorerBindingEditor opens the form when a TExplorerBinding is double clicked in the form designer, and EditExplorerBinding returns True if the bound controls were changed.
