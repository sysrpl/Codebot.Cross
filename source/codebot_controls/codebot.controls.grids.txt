# Codebot.Controls.Grids

The Codebot.Controls.Grids unit gives you TScrollWindow, a windowed control with scroll bars that draws an area larger than itself, and TContentGrid, a grid of cells you draw yourself in event handlers. When SingleColumn is True, a content grid acts as a list box. TGridCellManager keeps track of the column widths and row heights of a grid, and cells can span several columns and rows.

TImageListGridProvider shows the images of an image strip in a content grid, which is how the image strip editor shows its images.
