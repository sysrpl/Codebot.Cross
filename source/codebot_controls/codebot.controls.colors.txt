The Codebot.Controls.Colors unit defines controls that pick a color using the mouse.

- THuePicker selects a hue from either a bar or a color wheel.
- TSaturationPicker selects the saturation and lightness of a hue.
- TAlphaPicker selects the transparency of a color.
- TAnglePicker is a round dial with a hand that is dragged to pick an angle.
- TColorSlideEdit is a slide edit for a single component of a color.
