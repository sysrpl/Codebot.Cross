The Codebot.Controls.Extras unit defines a collection of useful controls.

- TDrawImage shows an image centered, fit, filled, stretched, or tiled, and can desaturate or colorize it.
- TDrawBox and TDrawPanel are a graphic control and a windowed control that draw nothing on their own and are instead drawn by an event handler.
- TIndeterminateProgress shows an animated status icon beside its caption while work is in progress.
- TStepBubbles shows the steps of a process, such as the pages of a wizard.
