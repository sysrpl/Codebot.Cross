# Codebot.Controls.Extras

The Codebot.Controls.Extras unit gives you a collection of useful controls.

| Control | Description |
| --- | --- |
| TDrawImage | Shows an image centered, fit, filled, stretched, or tiled, and can desaturate or colorize it. |
| TDrawBox | A graphic control that draws nothing on its own. Handle OnDraw to draw on its surface. |
| TDrawPanel | A windowed control that draws nothing on its own. Handle OnDraw to draw on its surface. |
| TIndeterminateProgress | Shows a status icon beside its caption, animating a busy icon while its status is psBusy. |
| TStepBubbles | Shows the steps of a process, such as the pages of a wizard, as a row of numbered arrows, highlighting the steps up to StepIndex. |
