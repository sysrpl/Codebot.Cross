# Codebot.Controls.Containers

The Codebot.Controls.Containers unit gives you controls that hold other controls.

TSizingPanel pads its edges, paints a background, and has a splitter the user can drag to resize it. You can double click it in the form designer to arrange its controls in a row.

TCaptionBox is a panel with a caption area along its top, painted in the caption colors of the system. It uses the active caption colors while a control inside it has the focus, and the inactive caption colors otherwise. Clicking the caption gives the focus to the first control inside the box, and it can show a close button.
