The Codebot.Graphics unit creates the drawing objects declared in Codebot.Graphics.Types. These include matrices, pens, solid, bitmap, and gradient brushes, fonts, surfaces for a canvas or a window, bitmaps, splash windows, and captures of the screen. Each object is made by the drawing backend of the platform, which is Cairo on Linux and Direct2D on Windows.

The unit also defines TSurfaceBitmap, which is a TGraphic that holds an IBitmap, and TImageStrip, which is a component that holds a series of images of the same size. Finally, it includes routines that fill and outline rectangles, along with TDefaultTheme and TRedmondTheme, which are the themes used to draw controls.
