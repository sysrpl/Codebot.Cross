The Codebot.Controls unit gives you the base classes for controls that draw on a surface. TSurfaceGraphicControl is a windowless control and TSurfaceCustomControl is a windowed one. Instead of overriding Paint, you override Draw, and Surface is ready for you while Draw runs. Each control has a DrawState that tracks whether it is hot, pressed, focused, or disabled, and a ThemeName that chooses the theme it is drawn with. If you want your control to redraw when the theme changes, override ThemeAware and return True. TSurfaceForm gives your forms the same abilities.

The unit also gives you TNotifyCollection<T>, a persistent collection that reports changes to its items, TEdgeOffset, which manages the padding or margin of a control, and ArrangeControls, which lays out the controls inside a container in a row.
