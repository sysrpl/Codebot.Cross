The Codebot.Render.NanoVG unit is a Pascal port of NanoVG by Mikko Mononen, an antialiased 2D vector drawing library rendered with OpenGL. It includes the OpenGL backend from nanovg_gl.h, which works with every desktop and embedded OpenGL version you can choose in render.inc. Text is rendered with Codebot.Render.FontStash, and images are loaded using NewBitmapData from Codebot.Platform. Strings are UTF-8.

Most of the time you will use the canvas of Codebot.Render.Graphics, which is built on this unit. NanoVG is licensed under the zlib license.
