# Codebot.Render.TrueType

The Codebot.Render.TrueType unit is a Pascal port of stb_truetype 1.26 by Sean Barrett. It ports the parts used to render fonts: loading fonts, looking up glyphs, metrics, kerning, glyph shapes for TrueType and CFF fonts, and the antialiased rasterizer. stb_truetype is public domain or MIT licensed.
