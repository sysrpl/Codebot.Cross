# Codebot.Render.World

The Codebot.Render.World unit gives you TWorld, a 2D world within a 3D space, which you reach through Ctx.World. It can draw a grid, and gives you a TCamera, which places a point of view in the world and moves it forward, backward, left, and right, and a TSkybox, a textured cube that faces inward and is drawn as the background.
