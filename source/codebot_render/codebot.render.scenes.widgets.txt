The Codebot.Render.Scenes.Widgets unit defines TWidgetScene, which is a scene that contains widgets, accessed through its Widget property. Input is sent to the widgets first and is passed on to the scene only when no widget handles it. To draw the widgets, call WidgetsRender from the Render method of the scene. The dialogs that are made from widgets are shown in the main widget of the first widget scene. The unit also defines TSimpleWidgetScene, a widget scene that needs no Render method. It draws the widgets itself each frame, and the first time it renders it places the widgets added to Widget in the middle of the scene, where a window can still be dragged. Its CloseEvent method can be given to the OnClick of a button or the OnClose of a window to close the program.
