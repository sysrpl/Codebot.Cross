The Codebot.Geometry unit gives you the types you need for 2D and 3D math.

- TVec2, TVec3, and TVec4 are vectors of two, three, and four components, which you build with the Vec, Vec2, Vec3, and Vec4 routines.
- TMatrix4x4 transforms points in 3D.
- TMatrix2x2 is a 2D matrix without translation, and TMatrix2x3 is a 2D affine matrix that can rotate, scale, skew, and translate.
- TQuaternion represents a rotation in 3D.
- TLine2 and TSlope2 describe 2D lines, and TBezier2 and TCurve2 are 2D cubic bezier curves.

Triangulate cuts a simple polygon into triangles.
