The Codebot.Cryptography unit computes hashes. HashString, HashBuffer, HashStream, and HashFile compute the MD5, SHA1, SHA256, or SHA512 digest of a string, memory, a stream, or a file, and AuthString, AuthBuffer, AuthStream, and AuthFile compute an HMAC digest of each using a key. DigestToStr turns a digest into text you can show or store.

The unit also gives you Encrypt and Decrypt, which obscure a string using a simple method. They are not secure, so don't use them to protect sensitive data.
