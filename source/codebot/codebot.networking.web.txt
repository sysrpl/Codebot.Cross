# Codebot.Networking.Web

The Codebot.Networking.Web unit makes HTTP requests.

- TUrl parses a URL into its parts, and converts to and from a string.
- WebGet and WebPost are the quickest way to make a request, writing the response to a stream or a string. CurlGet does the same using the curl program.
- WebSendRequest sends a request and reads the reply, including chunked replies.
- THttpClient is a reusable HTTP client, and THttpPost sends multipart post requests.

The unit also gives you routines that build GET and POST requests, parse response headers, encode and decode URLs and their arguments, and find the MIME type of a file.
